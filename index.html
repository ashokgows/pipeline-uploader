YouTube Pipeline Uploader

YouTube Pipeline Uploader is a personal-use desktop tool. It uploads videos that its operator has produced to YouTube channels that the same operator owns, and manages those channels' metadata (titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists and channel art) through the official YouTube Data API.

It is not a public service, is not distributed to other users, and does not process data belonging to anyone other than its operator.

This page documents the application that requests Google OAuth access. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
